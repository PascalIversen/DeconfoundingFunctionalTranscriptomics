  \caption{\textbf{Predictive accuracy: overall vs.\ within-tissue Pearson correlation.} Out-of-fold $r$ between the measured response and the model prediction, as mean\,$\pm$\,SEM across items ($n=200$ knockout targets for DepMap, $n=440$ drugs for CTRPv2); each item's value is first averaged over the five seeds, and all methods are evaluated on the same items and folds (paired). The within-tissue $r_{\mathrm{wt}}$ centres $y$ and the prediction within each tissue before correlating, removing the between-tissue (lineage) component. Overall $r$ is inflated by tissue structure: the tissue-mean baseline reaches substantial overall $r$ while carrying no within-tissue signal (its negative $r_{\mathrm{wt}}$ is the leave-one-fold-out anti-correlation of held-out tissue means). Every trained predictor sits far above the baseline in $r_{\mathrm{wt}}$, learning genuine within-tissue signal rather than collapsing to lineage. Residualization attains the highest overall \emph{and} within-tissue $r$ -- consistent with its model being trained directly on the within-tissue (residual) signal -- so deconfounding the data costs no predictive accuracy here; IRM is the weaker predictor on both axes. The tissue-mean baseline predicts the per-tissue mean response only. Within-tissue SHAP re-uses the marginal model ($\hat y_{\mathrm{within}}\equiv\hat y_{\mathrm{marginal}}$), so its predictive $r$ is identical to Marginal. For residualization the within-tissue $r_{\mathrm{wt}}$ is evaluated on the deconfounded model output $f(X_{\mathrm{res}})=\hat y_{\mathrm{res}}-\hat y_{\mathrm{baseline}}$; the raw reconstructed prediction adds the leave-out tissue mean back (needed only for overall $r$), whose negative within-tissue correlation would otherwise contaminate $r_{\mathrm{wt}}$ (giving a misleading 0.06 on CTRPv2, 0.16 on DepMap). Within-tissue $r_{\mathrm{wt}}$ is heterogeneous across items (SD $\approx$ 0.1; some items predict well, others near zero), but because all methods are compared on the same items, the small SEM reflects the consistent paired difference rather than low heterogeneity.
